Motor sport

What is the School Bus Race?

Ankara 2026

What is it?

A motor sport competition for the vehicle brands most preferred as school buses. They come together and race — the first one to reach Anka Bilim School wins.

The setting

It is raced on real streets and in large parking lots, just like a real motor sport. The finish line is Anka Bilim School. The rest of the year, these buses carry students across large parts of the city.

Where it began

The race was first organised by the school bus drivers' union to support bus drivers.

How to enter

You only need one of the six models on the grid. Same vehicles, different strengths, one finish line.
